Summer with the Twins – Youth Baseball Drills

## Summary of Experience
This project documents my summer working with the Minnesota Twins youth program. I helped run baseball drills all day, keeping kids engaged, organized, and moving. From skill stations to games, I managed groups, taught fundamentals, and kept the energy high while making baseball fun for everyone. It was a hands-on experience in leadership, teamwork, and communication.

## Tools and resources
- Scheduling and organization tools for daily drill rotations  
- Baseball equipment and training materials  
- Communication and coordination with staff and players  

## Files and materials
- Daily drill schedules  
- Attendance and activity tracking sheets  

## Program setup guide
1. Review the daily drill plan.  
2. Organize groups by age and skill level.  
3. Rotate through stations every 20–30 minutes.  
4. End with team games and cool-down activities.
